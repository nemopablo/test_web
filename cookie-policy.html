Cookie Policy

Summary

We only use analytics and advertising cookies if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Reject", or do not respond, none of them are set and the website works in exactly the same way.

Strictly necessary storage

We save one item called cookieConsent in your browser's local storage to remember whether you accepted or rejected cookies, so that we do not ask on every visit. It contains no personal information and does not require consent.

Analytics cookies (only with your consent)

Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* (kept for up to two years). They tell us how many people visit the site, which pages they view and whether they tap the call or WhatsApp buttons.

Advertising cookies (only with your consent)

Google Ads sets cookies such as _gcl_au and _gcl_aw (kept for up to 90 days). They tell us whether a visit or a call came from one of our adverts, so that we can measure how well the adverts work.

Changing your choice

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link, which is also in the footer of the home page. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

Third parties

The cookies above are provided by Google, which may process the information on servers outside the UK. See Google's privacy policy for details. If you tap the WhatsApp button you leave this website and WhatsApp's own terms apply.
