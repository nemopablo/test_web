Do you really come out at night and at weekends?

Yes. Our phone is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including bank holidays, and a locksmith is sent out as soon as we have your address.

How quickly can you get to me?

It depends on where you are and on traffic at the time. We are based in Croydon, so nearby areas are the quickest to reach. When you call we give you an estimated arrival time, and we let you know if anything changes.

How much will it cost?

The price depends on the type of lock and what needs doing, so we cannot give one figure for every job. We tell you the price before any work starts, and if the job turns out to need something extra we ask you first.

Will you have to break my lock or door?

We always try to open the door without damage first. Some locks cannot be opened that way, and in that case we explain what is needed and what it will cost before going ahead.

Do I need to prove that I live there?

We may ask for ID or another proof that you live at, own or are allowed to enter the property. If your documents are locked inside, you can show them once the door is open.

Which locks and doors do you work on?

All common types of lock, on wooden, composite, aluminium and UPVC doors. We also fit and repair window locks, home safes and master key systems for homes and businesses.
